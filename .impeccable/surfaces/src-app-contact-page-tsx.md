# Contact (/contact)

Scope: whole route. Mode: Persuade. Extends the established charcoal/off-white/coral world (Geist, Inter, Bricolage); no new identity.

Audience: small-business owners who've seen the work and are deciding whether to reach out. Action: send the form, or use a direct channel. Proof: remote, works anywhere (no city named). No response-time, pricing or client-count claims.

Constraints: form delivery undecided. Placeholder submit opens the visitor's mail app with a pre-filled message. Globe must be cheap on phones: canvas, precomputed mesh, no per-frame React state, paused off screen.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Remote, made tangible: a planet turns to face *your* time zone, and the conversation starts on its horizon. Refuses the default of a centered heading, a two-column contact card and a globe tucked in a corner.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal ground, a coral hairline wireframe globe (graticule at low off-white opacity, the visitor's 15° time-zone band washed in coral), off-white Geist display with a Bricolage coral accent word, quiet hairline inputs, coral pill submit, project-type chips.

STORY: The visitor sees we work wherever they are (the globe faces them, showing their local time). They believe reaching out is low-risk because they can see exactly what happens next. Then they send a message or tap email or phone.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The top third holds a big headline "Tell us about your business." with a short subline. The globe's upper hemisphere rises from the bottom edge, about 120vw wide on phones and 1100px on desktop. A line on the horizon reads "It's 9:14 pm where you are." The form card rises over the horizon line, and its top edge sits in the first viewport on desktop. The primary action is the form's coral submit.

FORM: Structure 6 of 7 (globe horizon), seed 026f5711. Signature interaction: the globe eases to the visitor's time-zone longitude on load, then drifts. Drag rotates it horizontally, and vertical touch still scrolls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
